Company Leave Policy

Employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave requests must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance.
Employees can carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year.

Work From Home Policy

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Employees must attend mandatory in-person meetings when requested by their manager.
Employees must wash hands.

IT Security Policy

Employees must never share their passwords with colleagues.
All company laptops must use approved security software.
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the IT security team immediately.
